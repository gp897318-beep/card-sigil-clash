✦ SIGIL CLASH ✦

A simultaneous-reveal card duel. Both sides commit one card face-down to the arena — then flip together. Higher power wins the clash and deals the damage.

HOW TO PLAY
1 · Each round, pick ONE card from your hand and commit it to the arena.
2 · The rival commits too — its hint tells you which sigil it favours. Counter it!
3 · Sigil wheel: Flame beats Gale, Gale beats Stone, Stone beats Tide, Tide beats Flame (+3 power).
4 · Winner deals damage = margin + sigil bonus. Win 3 clashes in a row for a Sigil Burst +6.
5 · Cards cost Focus (you gain +2 each round). First to 0 Life loses.
