A simultaneous-reveal card duel for the make.fun **best card game** bounty.

Both duelists commit one card face-down to the centre arena, then flip
together. Higher power wins the clash and deals the margin as damage. Four
sigils form a wheel — Flame beats Gale, Gale beats Stone, Stone beats Tide,
Tide beats Flame (+3 power). Win three clashes in a row to unleash a Sigil
Burst (+6 damage). The rival telegraphs its favoured sigil before you commit,
so every round is a readable counter-pick: read the hint, pick the counter,
manage your Focus.

- 30 Life each side · deck of 30 · hand of 6 · Focus costs ◆1–4
- Keyboard: 1–6 pick a card, Enter/Space commit, R restart, M mute
- Pure HTML/CSS/JS, no libraries, no network — all art drawn in code

Play locally: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080
